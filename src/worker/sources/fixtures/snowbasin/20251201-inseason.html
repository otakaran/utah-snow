Last Updated: Nov 30 3:17 PM MT

With warm weather lingering, we will be pushing back our Opening Day. Snowbasin will open the moment conditions allow, whether that's a weekday or weekend.

This is typical as we set ambitious targets. In recent years, we've opened early, on time, and delayed. We will always strive to open as early as possible to give you the best start to your season. 

"Our team is standing by, ready to act when the weather returns to a normal pattern. We have one of the most sophisticated snowmaking systems in the ski industry and can open quickly once temperatures drop," said Snowbasin COO Davy Ratchford.

We know you're as excited as we are to get the season started. We can't wait to welcome you back to the mountain and show you what we've accomplished this summer with the new Becker Lift, expanded beginner terrain, and RFID gates throughout the resort.

Until Opening Day, pick up your RFID season pass at the Grizzly Center Rental Desk on Saturdays and Sundays or the Meadow Yurt Monday through Friday from 10am to 3pm. On Saturdays and Sundays, you can also get your skis tuned and shop for the latest gear at the Grizzly Center. Pick up your pass early for a chance to win a winter lift ticket and a 26/27 Premier Pass. We can't wait to see you on the mountain!

Our Black Friday-Cyber Monday sale is happening now through Monday, December 1st! Stock up on holiday gifts and save big on skiing with early bird pricing on season passes, a discounted Basin Flex Card, and 4-Day Demo and Lesson Packages.
