Good Morning Riders!

Fresh snow is back after a long dry stretch, giving the mountain a much-needed refresh. The base sits at 39”, with light snow falling through late morning and early afternoon to keep surfaces soft and turning nicely.

Mostly cloudy with snow likely between 8am–2pm. Temps hover near 33–37°, light west-northwest winds, and a 70% chance of precipitation with up to an inch of new snow possible. A more active pattern continues into the week, so stay tuned.
